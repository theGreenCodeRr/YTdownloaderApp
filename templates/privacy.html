Privacy Policy — Simple Downloader

Package ID: com.quantumsofts.simpledownloader · Effective Date: November 14, 2026

Simple Downloader ("the App") helps you inspect and download publicly accessible media links to your device via a user-configured server (defaulting to https://yt.quantumsofts.com).

1. Data We Process

  • Media URLs you submit: Links you paste or share into the App are sent over HTTPS solely to fetch available media formats and prepare your requested download.
  • Anonymous Client Identifier (X-Client-ID): The App generates a random UUID locally on your device so your download queue and recent history remain isolated from other users. No account, email address, phone number, or personal profile is required.
  • Rate-Limiting Logs: For server stability and abuse prevention, incoming IP addresses are temporarily checked in memory for rate limiting and discarded automatically after the short rate-limit window expires.

2. Data Retention & Deletion

  • Temporary Media Files: Processed media and ZIP archives are automatically purged shortly after download completion or expiration.
  • 24-Hour History Expiry: Download history records are automatically filtered and expired after 24 hours, or deleted immediately whenever you tap the Clear button in the App.
  • Local Device Files: Downloaded files saved on your phone or tablet can be deleted at any time from inside Settings → Delete Files or by uninstalling the App.

3. No Tracking or Third-Party Sharing

Simple Downloader does not sell, rent, or share user data with advertisers or data brokers, and contains no third-party advertising SDKs or behavioral analytics trackers.

4. App Permissions

  • Network Access (INTERNET): Used to communicate with your configured Simple Downloader backend server.
  • App Storage: Used to save your requested media downloads and resume interrupted transfers.

5. Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, please contact support@quantumsofts.com.
